About Ngwino

Who We Are

Ngwino was created by innovative students from Rwanda Coding Academy who recognized a critical challenge in our financial system. Launched as a first-year student project, we've combined our technical skills with a deep understanding of local financial needs to develop a solution that works for everyday Rwandans.

What We Do

Our platform connects individuals seeking financial services with banks and financial institutions offering loans, investment opportunities in shares, and bonds with reasonable collateral requirements. We believe that access to capital shouldn't be determined by the assets you already own, but by your potential and creditworthiness.

Our Mission

At Ngwino, we're bridging the gap between Rwandan citizens and financial institutions. Our platform simplifies the process of securing loans, shares, and bonds without the burden of excessive collateral requirements that have traditionally limited access to financial services.
